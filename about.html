Why we started.

Filipino homes pay some of the highest electricity rates in Southeast Asia. Solar obviously makes sense here. Yet every homeowner we met was stuck at the same place: three quotes that did not agree, no way to check who was legitimate, and a phone that would not stop ringing once they asked a single question.

That is not a technology problem. It is a trust problem. So we built the thing we kept wishing existed: an independent first step that gives you the numbers before anyone gets your number.

How we got here.

From a classroom in Makati to a roof in Metro Manila, in about sixteen months.

The beginning
April 2025

Three of us met through the Asian Institute of Management's Master in Innovation and Business programme.

The idea
June 2025

We went looking at carbon markets and clean energy. CarbonConnect was born.

The validation
July 2025

We stopped guessing and went and asked. Dozens of conversations confirmed the same thing: going solar in the Philippines is confusing, and nobody trusts the quotes.

The prototype
September 2025

First working prototype. Rough, but it read a bill and produced a number.

The evolution
January to March 2026

CarbonConnect became Apolaki, and we were selected for the UNLEASH Prototyping Programme.

The first installation
March 2026

We onboarded our first verified installer partner and completed our first solar installation. The idea stopped being an idea.

The soft launch
April to July 2026

Soft launched the platform, expanded what it could do, and put up our website and Facebook page.

Demo day

Presenting Apolaki at the AIM MIB Demo Day.
