Our story

Making solar simple — and trustworthy — for every Filipino home.

We are a student venture from the Asian Institute of Management, building the clear, honest readiness layer that helps Filipino households decide on solar with confidence — and connects them to installers they can trust.

How we got here

From a question to demo day.

Apolaki is named after the Filipino god of the sun. The idea began as our team's first-stage manuscript, “CarbonConnect,” which named a problem we kept hearing in Filipino homes: people were not against solar — they were unsure. The barrier was investment and trust, not interest. We did not write a roadmap behind closed doors; we shipped, listened, and let real questions from homeowners and installers shape what came next.

  1. The question

    “CarbonConnect” — naming the real barrier

    Our first-stage manuscript found that Filipinos weren't against solar; they were unsure. The barrier was investment and trust, not interest — so that is what we set out to solve.

    Ideation photo — coming from the team

  2. Apolaki is born

    CarbonConnect becomes Apolaki, as VESS Corp.

    At the Asian Institute of Management (Master in Innovation & Business), the idea became Apolaki, operating as VESS Corp. — with one mission: remove the barriers that keep Filipinos from clean energy.

  3. Release 01

    Returns Calculator

    We answered the question everyone asked first — do the numbers even work for a house like mine? — proving the economics in plain peso terms before building anything else.

  4. Release 02

    Analysis Platform

    We added satellite assessment with NASA POWER irradiance and Google Solar rooftop data — so credibility comes from real irradiance and your own roof, not a generic average.

  5. Release 03

    Multi-role app

    The third release became a real product: sign-in, a consent-based onboarding that asks permission before anything is shared, and a light/dark theme so it feels at home on any device.

  6. Release 04

    Production on Google Cloud

    The current build runs on Google Cloud with bill-OCR onboarding — snap your electricity bill instead of typing — and the “Apolaki Intelligence” dashboard that turns your numbers into a decision.

  7. Next stop

    Demo Day · 29 August 2026

    We present Apolaki — the readiness engine, the app, and the installer network — built in the open across four public releases.

Why we exist

Mission & vision

The two statements we hold ourselves to.

Our mission

To remove the barriers that prevent Filipinos from adopting clean energy.

Our vision

A future where every Filipino can achieve energy independence through clean energy.

Our work is anchored to UN Sustainable Development Goal 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy, and supported by SDG 8 (decent work and economic growth) through the installers and livelihoods we help connect, and SDG 13 (climate action) through every roof that switches to the sun.

The barrier was never the sun — it was cost and trust. We exist to remove both.
What we stand for
